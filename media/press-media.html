KFM Condemns Continued Communications Restrictions in IOK
March 2026

The Kashmir Freedom Movement strongly condemns the Indian government's ongoing use of internet restrictions and communications blackouts in Indian Occupied Kashmir as a tool of collective punishment against the civilian population, in violation of international human rights law.

KFM Calls on UN Security Council to Enforce Resolution 47
February 2026

On the occasion of Kashmir Solidarity Day, the Kashmir Freedom Movement calls upon the United Nations Security Council to take immediate and concrete steps to enforce its own Resolution 47 of 1948, which guarantees the people of Jammu and Kashmir the right to determine their future through a free and fair plebiscite.

Statement on the 42nd Anniversary of Martyrdom of Maqbool Butt
February 2026

KFM marks the 42nd anniversary of the martyrdom of Shaheed Maqbool Butt — founder of the JKLF and the greatest son of Kashmir — and reaffirms commitment to the cause for which he gave his life: a free, independent, and united Jammu and Kashmir.

In the News

Media
Coverage

KFM's work and the Kashmir cause have been covered by leading international media outlets. A selection of recent coverage is featured below.

BBC World Service

KFM President Eng. Faiz Ullah Khan interviewed on BBC World Service discussing the UN's role in resolving the Kashmir dispute and the impact of Article 370's abrogation on Kashmiri civil society.

Al Jazeera English

Al Jazeera features KFM's annual rally in London and interviews KFM spokespeople on the prospects for a diplomatic resolution to the Kashmir issue in the current geopolitical climate.

The Guardian

An op-ed by KFM on the longest internet shutdown in democratic history — Kashmir's communications blackout — and its devastating impact on education, healthcare, and human rights documentation.

Dawn (Pakistan)

Dawn covers KFM's advocacy at the UN Human Rights Council and the organisation's growing international coalition of supporting NGOs and civil society organisations.
