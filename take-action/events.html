KFM Events

Conferences, rallies, commemorations, and community gatherings — KFM brings together people across the world to stand for Kashmir's freedom.

Annual Conference
KFM holds an annual international conference bringing together advocates, scholars, and political leaders to review progress and set the agenda for the year ahead.
Rallies & Protests
We organise public rallies and peaceful demonstrations in major cities — from London to Toronto — to raise visibility and public pressure for Kashmir's cause.
Commemorations
KFM marks key dates in Kashmir's history — including Martyrs' Day (13 July), Maqbool Butt Day (11 February), and Kashmir Solidarity Day (5 February).
Public Lectures
We regularly host public lectures, panel discussions, and educational seminars featuring scholars, former diplomats, and Kashmiri community voices.

Annual · London, UK
Kashmir Solidarity Day Rally 2026

Join thousands of Kashmiris and supporters in central London for our annual Kashmir Solidarity Day rally — marking Pakistan's national day of solidarity with the Kashmiri people. Featuring speeches, performances, and a march to the Indian High Commission.

11
February
Annual Commemoration · Multiple Cities
Maqbool Butt Martyrdom Day

On 11 February 1984, Maqbool Butt — founder of the Jammu Kashmir Liberation Front and Shaheed-e-Azam of Kashmir — was hanged by Indian authorities in New Delhi's Tihar Jail. KFM holds memorial gatherings worldwide to honour his sacrifice and reaffirm commitment to Kashmir's freedom.

13
July
Annual Commemoration
Kashmir Martyrs' Day 2026

Commemorating 13 July 1931, when 22 Kashmiris were shot by the Dogra Maharaja's forces during a protest — the event that sparked modern Kashmiri political consciousness. KFM holds memorial events and community gatherings to remember the martyrs and reaffirm the struggle.

Past Events

Our
History of Action

KFM has been organising events for decades — from small community gatherings to international conferences attended by hundreds.

Annual Conference 2025

KFM held its 2025 Annual Conference in Birmingham, UK, bringing together 200+ delegates from 12 countries to discuss advocacy strategy and the road to a plebiscite.

European Tour 2024

KFM organised a series of public meetings and parliamentary briefings across Germany, France, Belgium, and the Netherlands in 2024 to raise Kashmir awareness in the European Union.

UN Geneva Advocacy 2024

A KFM delegation participated in the UN Human Rights Council session in Geneva, presenting a report on human rights conditions in Indian Occupied Kashmir.

UK Diaspora Assembly 2023

The first KFM UK Diaspora Assembly, bringing together Kashmiri community organisations from across Britain to coordinate a unified advocacy strategy.
