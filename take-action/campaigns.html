Our Campaigns

From petition drives and social media movements to lobbying at the United Nations — KFM's campaigns are designed to translate awareness into action, and action into change.

Active Campaigns How to Participate
Home›Take Action›Campaigns
Awareness
We raise the profile of Kashmir's cause through targeted social media campaigns, international media outreach, and public education initiatives.
Petitions
We organise mass petition drives directed at the UN Security Council, foreign governments, and international human rights bodies to demand action on Kashmir.
Lobbying
KFM representatives engage directly with governments, parliaments, and international organisations to advocate for Kashmiri self-determination.
Solidarity
We build coalitions with like-minded movements, human rights organisations, and diaspora communities to amplify Kashmir's voice on the world stage.

#FreeKashmir — UN Plebiscite Now
Active

Our flagship global campaign demanding the United Nations enforce its own resolutions on Kashmir — particularly UN Security Council Resolution 47 (1948) — and facilitate a free and fair plebiscite for the people of Jammu and Kashmir. Join millions of voices calling for the promise of the UN to be kept.

Restore Article 370 — End Direct Rule
Active

On 5 August 2019, India unilaterally abrogated Article 370 of its constitution, stripping Jammu and Kashmir of its special autonomous status and converting it into a centrally governed Union Territory. This campaign demands the restoration of Kashmiri political rights and an end to direct rule imposed without consent.

End the Communications Blackout
Active

Kashmir has endured the longest internet shutdown in the history of any democracy. This campaign documents and campaigns against the systematic use of communications blackouts as a tool of collective punishment — demanding the Indian government restore and maintain full access to internet and telecommunications for all residents of Jammu and Kashmir.

How to
Get Involved

Every voice counts. Here is how you can actively participate in KFM campaigns from wherever you are in the world.

Share & Amplify

Share campaign content on social media using our hashtags. Every share reaches new audiences and adds to the pressure on decision-makers.

Sign Petitions

Sign and circulate our petitions to the UN and governments. Petition signatures are a powerful signal to politicians and international bodies.

Write to Officials

Use our campaign templates to write to your local MP, senator, or government representative demanding action on Kashmir.

Join as a Member

Become a KFM member to access campaign resources, get action alerts, and join campaign working groups making the decisions.
