Every hour you give directly advances the cause of 14 million people. Volunteering with KFM is not just giving back — it is making history.
Skill Development
Gain real experience in advocacy, communications, research, event management, and international relations — skills that will last a lifetime.

Volunteer Roles

Find Your
Role

KFM needs people with all kinds of skills. Browse our current volunteer openings below.

Writer & Editor

Write blog posts, press releases, reports, and campaign materials. Help tell the story of Kashmir's cause to the world.

Graphic Designer

Create campaign visuals, infographics, social media content, and print materials that communicate our message with impact.

Social Media Manager

Manage KFM's social channels, grow our audience, and run digital campaigns on Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and YouTube.

Translator

Help translate KFM content into Urdu, Kashmiri, Pahari, Arabic, French, German, and other languages to reach wider audiences.

Public Speaker

Represent KFM at community events, conferences, university panels, and media appearances as an official spokesperson.

Researcher

Conduct policy research, monitor human rights reports, compile UN documentation, and support KFM's advocacy with evidence.

Event Organiser

Plan and manage KFM events — from local community meetings to national conferences and international delegations.

Web & Tech

Maintain and develop KFM's digital platforms, website, and online tools — helping us reach and engage more people effectively.
