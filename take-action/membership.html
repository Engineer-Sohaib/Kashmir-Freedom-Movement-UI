Common questions about our platform and how to get involved.

5 Questions Answered
KF Movement is a community-driven initiative dedicated to empowering individuals, advocating for positive change, and fostering growth through education, activism, and social impact — centered on Kashmir's right to self-determination.
You can join KFM by volunteering, participating in events, supporting campaigns, or donating to help further our mission. Fill out the membership form above and our team will reach out within 48 hours.
We focus on education, community development, social justice, mental health awareness, and humanitarian aid. At the core of all our work is the cause of Kashmir's self-determination in accordance with UN resolutions.
Yes! Donations help us fund initiatives, provide resources, and expand our reach. Visit our donation page to contribute securely. Every contribution — no matter the size — makes a meaningful difference.
Follow us on Facebook, Twitter, YouTube, and Instagram, subscribe to our newsletter, or regularly check our website to stay informed about events, campaigns, and impact stories from across Kashmir.

The Kashmir Freedom Movement is working for the integration, mobilization, and empowerment of the people of Jammu and Kashmir towards the self-determination cause.
