Your Contribution

Every Donation
Fuels the
Movement

The Kashmir Freedom Movement operates entirely through the generosity of supporters like you. There are no corporate sponsors, no government funding — only people who believe in the right of 14 million Kashmiris to determine their own future.

Your donation — large or small — directly funds our work on the ground and across the world. It pays for campaigns, publications, events, and the infrastructure that keeps the movement connected across continents.

Global Advocacy Campaigns
Organizing demonstrations, lobbying governments, and engaging international forums to push for Kashmir's self-determination.
Research & Publications
Producing reports, books, and educational materials that document Kashmir's history, rights violations, and legal case.
Youth & Leadership Programs
Training the next generation of Kashmiri advocates through workshops, mentorship, and skills development.
Digital & Media Outreach
Maintaining our website, social media, and digital platforms that keep millions informed and connected to the cause.
Commemorations & Events
Organizing annual events to honor martyrs like Shaheed Maqbool Butt and keep the memory of the struggle alive.
Our Promise
100% of your donation goes directly to KFM's mission. We are a volunteer-driven organization with minimal overhead — your generosity is maximized for the cause.
