Executive Council

Our
Leadership

KFM is guided by a committed executive council — experienced advocates, community organisers, and scholars who have dedicated their lives to the cause of Kashmiri self-determination.

President
Eng. Faiz Ullah Khan

Founder and President of the Kashmir Freedom Movement. An engineer by profession and a lifelong advocate for Kashmiri self-determination, Eng. Khan has led KFM since its establishment, building it into a respected international organisation that represents the voice of millions.

General Secretary
Muhammad Shafique Kayani

General Secretary of KFM, responsible for coordinating operations, managing communications between chapters worldwide, and leading advocacy outreach to international bodies, governments, and the United Nations.

Senior Vice President
Abdul Hamid

Senior Vice President and a key strategist within KFM. Abdul Hamid oversees regional chapter development and community mobilisation efforts across Pakistan-administered Kashmir and the wider global diaspora.

Organisational Structure

How KFM
is Organised

The Kashmir Freedom Movement operates through a democratic, multi-tiered structure designed to ensure accountability, broad participation, and effective advocacy at every level. The organisation is governed by an elected Executive Council, supported by regional coordinators, working committees, and an advisory board of scholars and legal experts.

Regional chapters operate across Azad Kashmir, Gilgit-Baltistan, the UK, Europe, North America, and the Kashmiri diaspora worldwide. Each chapter is empowered to run local campaigns, events, and community outreach, while operating under the unified vision and charter of the central organisation.

Executive Council

Elected leadership body responsible for strategic direction, policy, and representing KFM at international forums and the United Nations.

Regional Chapters

Active chapters across AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan, UK, Europe, and North America driving grassroots action and community engagement.

Working Committees

Specialised committees for media, legal affairs, youth, research, and humanitarian outreach operating under the executive council.

Advisory Board

A panel of scholars, legal experts, and senior advocates providing guidance on strategy, international law, and political engagement.
