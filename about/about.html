About Us

Our Mission &
Declaration

We the members of Kashmir Freedom Movement (KFM) believe that, "To oppress and do injustice to others is sin — but to accept the life of oppression and injustice is an even greater sin."

The people of the State of Jammu & Kashmir, once an independent and sovereign nation, have been subjected to oppression, subjugation, and forced division since 27 October 1947. KFM exists to empower and lead the people in their struggle for peace, social justice, freedom, and re-unification.

We seek to regain our sovereignty and independence and re-establish our status as a nation state in accordance with the Charter of the United Nations — so that we can live in peace and harmony within the community of nations.

1
Jammu Kashmir belongs to all who live in it without distinction of colour, caste, creed, region, or religion.
2
It is not a territorial dispute between India and Pakistan — it is a question of the right of self-determination of 14 million people.
3
India and Pakistan should withdraw simultaneously all their military, para-military, and other forces from Jammu Kashmir.
4
All human rights violations at the hands of armed forces and their backed militants must end now.
5
Freedom of speech and movement of civilians, political and humanitarian workers should be allowed throughout Jammu Kashmir without restriction.
6
The United Nations should facilitate free and fair elections for a people's representative Council from all regions to negotiate the final settlement.
7
The international community should facilitate a peace process so Kashmiris can determine their national status through a free and fair plebiscite.

KFM in Action

Members advocating for Kashmir's freedom worldwide

Mission
Kashmir Freedom Movement exists to empower and lead the people in their struggle for peace, social justice, freedom, and re-unification of the state of Jammu and Kashmir.
Vision
A just, independent, and modern welfare state for the people of Jammu and Kashmir — where all citizens live in dignity, freedom, and harmony regardless of religion, ethnicity, or background.
Appeal
We appeal to all freedom-loving, conscientious, and humanist nations, individuals, and organizations to support KFM's aims — and help us achieve our mission: peace, social justice, and freedom.
