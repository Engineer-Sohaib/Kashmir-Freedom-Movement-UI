Aksai Chin

A Detailed Page Is Coming

Aksai Chin is the easternmost portion of the Kashmir region, currently administered by China following the 1962 Sino-Indian War. An arid high-altitude plateau at the edge of the Tibetan Plateau, it remains one of the least-known parts of the Kashmir conflict. We are preparing a comprehensive page documenting its geography, history, and geopolitical significance.
